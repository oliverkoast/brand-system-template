# Hotelink — Voice & Copy Guide

How Hotelink writes. Claude reads this whenever it writes or edits copy. It governs **voice**
(how we say it), not **facts** (what we say). Real details always come from the person.

> Source: the live copy on hotelink.ai, captured 2026-10-30 (see `source/hotelink-site.md`).
> The principles below are read off that copy, not supplied by Hotelink. Confirm with the team.

## The voice in one line

Plain-spoken and sure of itself. Talks like an operator, not a vendor.

## What Hotelink is (the one sentence to reuse)

A managed data platform for independent and mid-size hotels: every system the hotel runs, in
one warehouse Hotelink operates, so teams can build their own dashboards and apps without a
data team. It sits **alongside** the PMS and other operating systems. It does not replace them.

## Tone principles

- **Short, declarative sentences.** "Your hotel. Connected." "Careful by default."
- **The operator's question, in the operator's words.** Lead with what a GM, revenue manager,
  or director of sales actually asks, not with platform features.
- **Candid about limits.** The site says when a hotel's PMS reports are already enough. Keep
  that honesty: never claim Hotelink replaces a system or does something it does not.
- **Trust is stated plainly.** Data safety is a promise in one line, not a paragraph of
  reassurance.
- **No data-team jargon.** No "single source of truth", "synergy", "unlock", "seamless",
  "revolutionary", "AI-powered" as a selling line.

## Rhythm

- Headlines are **2 to 9 words**. Observed: "Your hotel. Connected." (3) through
  "Every system your hotel runs, finally in one place." (9).
- The two-beat headline (noun phrase, full stop, payoff) is the signature move. Use it, do not
  overuse it: at most one per piece.
- **No em-dashes.** Use commas, colons, or full stops.

## Name

The site sets the name lowercase: **hotelink**. In running copy written by others (press,
decks) "Hotelink" appears too. See `qa/FINDINGS.md` H-03 before choosing.

## Calls to action

Invitational and specific. Approved, from the site: **"Book a walkthrough"**, **"Sign in"**.
Never "Learn more" or "Get started", which say nothing about where the reader is going.

## What never gets invented

- Names, dates, figures, prices, quotes, results, customer names.
- **Any partner or integration claim.** Only systems listed in `source/hotelink-site.md` may be
  named as connected, and "partner" vs "integration" is a question for Hotelink (H-08).
- Anything characterising Proper Hospitality beyond "Shaped inside Proper Hospitality."

If a detail is missing, use a labeled placeholder and ask.
